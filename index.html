Nothing is signed. At the time of writing, Enzo Fernández is a Chelsea player and no transfer to Manchester City has been agreed or announced. This page is a shell, published early, and every figure on it is blank until a deal is confirmed.

The deal. Manchester City announced the signing of Enzo Fernández from Chelsea on 1 September 2026, on a five-year contract keeping him at the Etihad Stadium until the summer of 2031. Expiry here is set to 30 June 2031, the standard end date for a Premier League contract.

The fee. David Ornstein reported a package worth £125m, matching the British transfer record, after City opened at £104m plus add-ons and Chelsea asked for £135m guaranteed. The per-season figure above is that fee spread evenly across the five seasons of the contract, which is roughly how a club books it.

The wages are not confirmed. No salary has been disclosed by either club. This page uses a placeholder of £400,000 a week, and every wage total on it — the tickers, the rates, the combined figure — is only as good as that assumption. Treat it as an illustration, not a report.

The money is an estimate. Clubs do not publish salaries. The figure used here comes from press reporting and wage trackers, and should be read as an approximation rather than a confirmed number.

Per-game figure assumes a 38-match Premier League season. Totals run in real time from the clock on your device.
